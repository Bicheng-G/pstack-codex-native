# eval

Pin the behavior being evaluated, baseline and candidate prompts/skills, representative inputs, grading criteria, and a bounded cost/time budget. Keep evaluation artifacts isolated from the product working tree.

Use blind evaluation where meaningful: graders receive raw outputs and rubric without the author's intended answer. Hold task/environment constant, record failures/dropouts, and distinguish same-model repetitions from independent cross-model evidence. Inspect artifacts, not just summaries. Report denominator, outcomes, costs when known, and transfer limits. Do not call a paid provider without applicable authorization.
